# FMCG Sales & Profitability Analysis (2023–2025)

## 1. Executive Summary
This project involved a comprehensive **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a multi-year FMCG dataset containing over **18,000 records**. The goal was to identify seasonal sales patterns and evaluate the profitability of different product categories using Python-based data visualization.

## 2. Technical Tools
* **Language:** Python
* **Libraries:** Pandas (Data Cleaning), Matplotlib (Visualization)
* **Environment:** Google Colab

## 3. Key Insights (The "Profitability Paradox")
* **Top Revenue Generator:** **Beverages** lead the market in total sales volume and revenue.
* **Profitability Leader:** Despite lower sales volume, **Personal Care** and **Dairy & Breakfast** have the highest average profit margins.
* **The Paradox:** Beverages, while being the top seller, have the **lowest** profit margin among all categories.
* **Seasonality:** A massive sales surge is observed every **December**, followed by a sharp decline in November.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations
* **Marketing Shift:** The company should reallocate a portion of the marketing budget from Beverages to **Personal Care** to capitalize on higher margins.
* **Inventory Planning:** Increase stock levels for high-volume goods in Q4 to meet the December peak.
